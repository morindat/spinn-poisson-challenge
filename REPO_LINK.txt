What is in this ZIP
-------------------

                     imports.  Self-contained apart from numpy.

results_note.pdf     the 3-page results note: public metrics with five-run statistics,
                     a convergence/stability check, runtime, limitations and citations

benchmark/           unmodified copy of the public evaluator and case generator
experiments/         scripts that produced every number and figure in the note
results/             JSON/CSV outputs and figures referenced by the note
report/              LaTeX source of the results note
Makefile             convenience targets: test, robustness, stress, convergence,
                     figures, note, zip


Trained weights
---------------
None.  The method is a deterministic numerical solver (Chebyshev–Gauss–Lobatto
spectral collocation with a tensor-product direct solve), so there is nothing to
train, load or ship.  Every answer is computed from the case inputs at call time.


Expected behaviour on the official harness
------------------------------------------
Stage 1 (reference case, 129 x 129, five runs):
    MAE 2.93e-13   max|err| 7.95e-13   rel L2 6.88e-13   BC MSE 0
    all four targets passed by 8-9 orders of magnitude
Hidden-like suites (240 cases, 20 self-chosen seeds):
    worst rel L2 1.21e-11, BC MSE 0, Stage-3 composite S = -7.50 (the floor)
Runtime: 0.13 s for the reference case; ~1.5 s mean and 6.2 s worst per case,
against a 20-minute-per-case CPU budget.
